Founder & applications

MR

MarkovReserve

Individual claims reserving

Semi-Markov individual claims reserving engine for non-life insurance. It follows claim trajectories through IBNR, RBNP, RBNS, Closed0 and Closed+ states, estimates reserves by simulation, computes one-year CDR and compares results with Chain-Ladder, Cape Cod and Bornhuetter-Ferguson.

Open appGitHub

PensionView.be

Employee Benefits reporting

Platform for automated actuarial reporting for Employee Benefits teams. It aims to make reporting workflows more efficient, structured and accessible for professionals working with pension and benefits information.
